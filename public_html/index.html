FAQ
Are the diabetes games free?

Yes. Every game is free, with no sign-up and no adverts. Your scores are saved only in your own browser.

Who are they for?

Kids and teens with diabetes, their parents and carers, grandparents, babysitters, school staff and anyone who wants to learn about diabetes.

Can I use this as diabetes training for school staff or carers?

Yes, as a quick refresher. The Symptom Sorter and A Day with Sam cover spotting and treating hypos, highs and ketones. It isn’t an accredited course and doesn’t replace training from a diabetes team.

What will I learn?

How to spot and treat a hypo, what to do about highs and ketones, sick-day rules, exercise, carb counting, food choices and the glycaemic index.

Is this medical advice?

No. The games are for learning only. Always follow the care plan and advice from your own diabetes team.
